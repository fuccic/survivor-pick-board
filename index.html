How we pick

Up to 3 lives; a loss costs one Draws safe, or counted as a loss Each team once per half (rounds 1–19)
  1. Only a loss hurts.When draws are safe, every team is judged on its chance of losing, not its chance of winning, so a dogged team that draws a lot can beat a flashy one. If your pool counts draws as losses, switch the setting above and the page judges teams on their chance of not winning instead.
  2. Start from the market.This week's chances come straight from bookmaker prices, with the bookies' margin taken out. Prices already reflect injuries, form and team news.
  3. Rate every team for later rounds.Team strengths are fitted to the last three rounds of prices (recent weeks count more), lightly pulled toward Opta's season projection so one odd price can't swing a rating. Those strengths give a win, draw and loss chance for every future fixture.
  4. Plan the whole half, not just this week.Each team you have left goes to at most one round from now to round 19, arranged to keep you alive as long as possible. The safest team this week is not always the right pick if it's your only good option for a hard round later.
  5. Rank this week by the full plan.For each option, we lock it in now and rebuild the best plan around it. "Plan cost" is how many expected rounds of survival you give up compared with the best start.
  6. Stress-test before trusting it.The top pick is re-checked with a stronger and weaker home advantage and 60 runs with random noise on every team's rating. A pick that wins most of these is solid; a split result means it's a close call.
  7. Refresh every week.The odds and ratings are updated before each round. Your picks are saved in this browser, so next week just mark the team you used.
