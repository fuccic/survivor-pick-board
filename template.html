How we pick

  1. Only a loss hurts.Draws are free in this pool, so every team is judged on its chance of losing, not its chance of winning. A dogged team that draws a lot can be a better pick than a flashy one.
  2. Start from the market.This week's chances come straight from bookmaker prices, with the bookies' margin taken out. Prices already reflect injuries, form and team news.
  3. Rate every team for later rounds.Team strengths are fitted to the last three rounds of prices (recent weeks count more), lightly pulled toward Opta's season projection so one odd price can't swing a rating. Those strengths give a win, draw and loss chance for every future fixture.
  4. Plan the whole half, not just this week.Each unused team goes to at most one round from now to round 19, arranged to keep you alive as long as possible. The safest team this week is not always the right pick if it is the only good option you have for a hard round later.
  5. Rank this week by the full plan.For each option, we lock it in now and rebuild the best plan around it. "Plan cost" is how many expected rounds of survival you give up compared with the best start.
  6. Stress-test before trusting it.The top pick is re-checked with a stronger and weaker home advantage, more and less weight on Opta, and 60 runs with random noise on every rating. A pick that wins most of these is solid; a split result means it's a close call.
  7. Refresh every week.After each round: update results and lives, pull the new prices, refit, and replan. Ask Claude for "this week's survivor pick" to refresh this page.
